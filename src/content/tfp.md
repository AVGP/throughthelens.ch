{% timeline %}
{% step "01", "Before the Shoot" %}
We work together to design the session. We agree on the style of photos, coordinate a convenient time and location in the Zurich area, and develop a customized concept and moodboard that excites both of us.
{% endstep %}
{% step "02", "During the Shoot" %}
The shooting takes approximately **2 hours** and is completely free of charge. We'll work collaboratively to bring our agreed moodboard and concept to life in a relaxed, friendly environment.
{% endstep %}
{% step "03", "After the Shoot" %}
You will receive a collection of fully retouched, high-resolution digital photos for your personal portfolio and social media. In return, I gain the rights to showcase these photos in my professional portfolio and social channels.
{% endstep %}
{% endtimeline %}

{% card "Model Checklist", "How to prepare for our shoot:", "checklist-card" %}
{% checklist %}
- **Wardrobe & Makeup:** Bring clothes and makeup options that align with the moodboard and concept we agreed upon. Multiple outfits are welcome!
- **Be on Time:** Please arrive on time so we can make full use of the light and locations. If you run into delays, let me know as early as possible.
- **Communication:** Share your ideas and references! A great TfP shoot thrives on mutual energy and clear communication.
{% endchecklist %}
{% endcard %}
